Generative AI has collapsed the time it takes to summarise a hundred articles. It has done nothing to make those articles worth summarising. That asymmetry defines the current state of market intelligence : synthesis is now fast, but the output inherits every weakness of its inputs, and an analysis built on unverifiable sources cannot be defended to a decision-maker.
